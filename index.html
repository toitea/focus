Cash App / Block
Dec 2019–Feb 2025 · Risk Operations Disputes Specialist → Executive Relations Escalation Specialist

What if we made the process better?

At Cash App, I went from investigating payment disputes to handling executive-level escalations. I started noticing that some of the same problems kept coming back. Rather than just resolving another case, I independently created a solution and presented it to leadership.

It was adopted across the department, helping improve resolution times and reduce escalations and BBB complaints. I also contributed to emerging scam identification, suspicious caller tracking and claims backlog improvements.

Recognized for: initiative, leadership and going above and beyond.
03
DoorDash
Apr 2026–Present · Fraud Insights Operations Analyst

AI can be powerful. It can also be wrong.

I review false-positive cases daily, using AI, ML and LLM tools alongside analytical resources. What interests me is understanding why a decision was made and whether the evidence actually supports it.

Within a regulatory initiative, I independently identified improvement opportunities and worked with Engineering on changes. The project achieved a measurable quarterly reduction in false positives. I also support BPO teams, share investigative methods and contribute to authorized account review workflows.

When I analyze delivery patterns, I consider alternative explanations and report evidence rather than jumping to a fraud conclusion. For me, DoorDash's philosophy of getting 1% better every day means staying curious and sharing what I learn.

Recognized for: curiosity · 10+ implemented improvements contributed to.
